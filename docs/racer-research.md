# Elite racer research and product lessons

This note separates verified results and direct advice from product inferences. Relic Lens is not affiliated with, trained by, or endorsed by any player below.

## Ben / Darkee / Lightee7

Grinding Gear Games describes Ben as a multi-title HCSSF racer. In its 2022 interview, his concrete advice is to record gameplay, stop dying first, and analyze mistakes because most deaths were avoidable. His results include first Awakener in the 2019 event, first in the Maven event, and first plus multiple bonus bosses in Siege of the Atlas.

Product lesson: a serious item tool needs explicit safety floors and explainable failures, not only a blended score. This informed the Hardcore/Gauntlet required-stat gate.

## tie23he

GGG's ExileCon recap confirms tie23he won the 2019 Race Finale over Havoc and Waggle. A verified 3.8 Act 5 run is listed at 59:28. The commonly repeated one-life-flask anecdote was not strong enough to encode as fact.

Product lesson: campaign scoring should value movement and immediately usable power, but flask-slot optimization requires a future whole-character view and cannot be inferred from one item.

- https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2679082/page/1
- https://www.speedrun.com/poe/runs/y41656dy

## Havoc616

Havoc qualified for and placed second in the 2019 ExileCon final. His verified 3.17 runs cover many classes and archetypes. A community-written frame-by-frame analysis of his 2:31:25 Kitava run repeatedly records movement-speed boot checks, planned colors/links, selective identification, vendor timing and minimized town time.

Product lesson: race value is stage-dependent and opportunity-cost driven. Relic Lens now has a Campaign Race approach; PoE 1-only socket/link tricks are deliberately not transferred to PoE 2 scoring.

## AlkaizerX

Official results place Alkaizer second in the Maven HC SSF event, where he also took first Awakener, and second overall in the 2023 Trial of the Ancestors boss event on Juggernaut. Early Gauntlet standings also placed him third.

Product lesson: melee items should retain a coherent damage package without sacrificing the defence identity required to stay in contact range. Hardcore mode therefore increases life/base-defence/resistance weights while reducing luxury rarity and marginal offense.

## imexile / nickexile11

GGG confirms imexile won an ExileCon 2023 qualifier, and official Sentinel results place nickexile11 second across all seven Uber bosses. Community standings record an overall Ember Class Gauntlet win. Current official Rapture Gauntlet statistics also place nickexile11 among the fastest campaign completions.

Product lesson: practiced speed and completion safety are not opposites. The app keeps Campaign Race and Hardcore/Gauntlet as selectable approaches rather than declaring one universal racer score.

## Implemented and next

Implemented:

- Balanced, Campaign Race, and Hardcore/Gauntlet evaluation approaches.
- Stage-scaled targets, slot relevance, required-stat safety gates and visible failure reasons.
- Movement/offense weighting for campaign races and defensive weighting for Gauntlet profiles.

The next high-value feature is whole-character comparison: equipped-item deltas, total resistance/attribute/Spirit deficits, sustain and flask pressure. Those decisions cannot be judged honestly from an isolated item.
