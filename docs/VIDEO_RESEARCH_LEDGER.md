Policy: a video is discovery evidence until its mechanical claim is corroborated by a current GGG source or reproducible current-patch test.

## Evidence states

- `direct`: the referenced video and the cited interval were inspected.
- `indexed`: a build page or chapter index exposes the interval, but the original footage was not reliably inspectable in this environment.
- `unverified`: useful lead only; no claim may enter scoring.

## Research constraint

This environment did not provide a dependable way to watch every linked YouTube frame or recover transcripts with stable timestamps. Consequently, no entry above is represented as a directly watched mechanical demonstration, and no formula, uptime, DPS, AI behaviour or transition rule was imported from it. Future direct review must record the original YouTube URL, publication date, exact interval, visible character sheet/tree/items, and whether the result is reproducible on the live patch.
